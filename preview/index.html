About me

A developer who has run the whole launch, not just the code.

For three years I built and maintained client websites at Highupweb, a digital agency in Yaoundé. I led projects for an e-commerce store, a medical-equipment supplier, a training academy, a driving school and an education consultancy.

That meant more than writing HTML and CSS. I worked with designers and business owners, fixed what broke in older browsers, made slow pages faster, and took 15+ sites through hosting, deployment and launch day.

How we'll work

A clear process, from first call to launch

You always know what happens next, what it costs and when you'll see it.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We talk about your business, your customers and what the site must achieve. You get a written scope and a fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Structure & design

    I map the pages and content, then show you the key layouts on desktop and mobile before building.

  3. 03

    Build & test

    I build with clean, responsive code and test on real phones and browsers. You review on a private preview link.

  4. 04

    Launch & care

    I set up the domain, hosting and SSL, launch, and stay available for fixes, updates and improvements.
