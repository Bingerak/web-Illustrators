AfrikLink Digital

A French-language website for a digital marketing agency that helps African businesses grow online.

Client
AfrikLink Digital
My role
Web developer
Context
Freelance project
Platform
WordPress + Elementor
Live site
Visit website (opens in a new tab)

Objective

Explain a wide range of marketing services to small and medium-sized businesses in Cameroon and beyond, show transparent starting prices, and turn visitors into enquiries.

My role

Web development for the site: page layouts, service and pricing sections, and responsive behaviour across devices.

Approach

  1. A confident hero that states the benefit in one line, with the agency's email visible in the header.
  2. Around a dozen service cards, from community management and content creation to websites, visual identity and AI automation.
  3. Pricing packs in FCFA with a custom-quote tier, so business owners can see where to start.
  4. Team profiles, client testimonials, a case-studies page, a blog and a WhatsApp button to build trust and start conversations.
