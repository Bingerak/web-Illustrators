Ebikes&Gear

An online store for a UK retailer of premium electric bikes, road and mountain bikes, and cycling gear.

Client
Ebikes&Gear
My role
Web developer
Context
Freelance project
Platform
JavaScript single-page app
Live site
Visit website (opens in a new tab)

Objective

Sell high-value bikes to riders across the UK and Europe, from a Canterbury showroom, and make it easy to ask questions before paying for a bike that costs thousands of pounds.

My role

Web development for the store: page layouts, product browsing and responsive behaviour across phones and desktops.

Approach

  1. A bold, dark hero with the season's collection, a red accent drawn from the brand, and two clear paths: shop the collection or explore road bikes.
  2. An announcement bar for stock and delivery news, plus a currency switcher for pounds, euros and dollars.
  3. Category menus for road, mountain, e-bikes, gravel, framesets, Sur-Ron electric bikes and components, and a separate menu for repairs, servicing and rentals.
  4. A WhatsApp button in the header, wishlist, account and cart, and a showroom section with address, hours and directions.

What the site includes

Multi-currency (GBP, EUR, USD)Category and services menusWhatsApp sales contactWishlist, account and cart
