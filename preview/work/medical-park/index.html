Medical Park Hospital Equipment

A bilingual product catalogue for a supplier of hospital and laboratory equipment.

Client
Medical Park
My role
Project lead
Delivered
Highupweb, 2021–2024
Live site
Visit website (opens in a new tab)

Objective

Present a wide range of specialist equipment so hospitals and clinics can find products by department, in English or French.

My role

Project lead within the Highupweb team: coordinated delivery and built the frontend.

The site was designed and delivered by the Highupweb agency team in Yaoundé.

Approach

  1. Products grouped by department: imaging, medical furniture, diagnostics, consumables, laboratory and hygiene.
  2. Prominent search with an "all categories" filter for buyers who know the product they need.
  3. An English/French language switch for an international and local audience.
  4. A blog and "about us" section to build trust with institutional buyers.

What the site includes

Bilingual English / FrenchDepartment-based catalogueProduct search and cartBlog for credibility
